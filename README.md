## Overview

AlchemistNPC adds several new NPC vendors to Terraria who sell potion ingredients, rare crafting materials, and convenience items that are normally only obtainable through grinding specific enemies or biomes. The Alchemist, Jeweler, Operator, and Brewer NPCs move in when their respective boss-progression conditions are met.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **Terraria AlchemistNPC** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **New NPC Vendors** | Alchemist, Jeweler, Operator, and Brewer NPCs with unique inventories |
| **Potion Materials** | Buys herb seeds and potion ingredients reducing farming requirements |
| **Progression Gating** | NPCs unlock their inventories as you defeat bosses through the game |
| **Convenience Items** | Rare crafting materials and accessories available for Coins |
